Hey there 👋 I’m Anup, a software engineer based in Wollongong, NSW, Australia, with over a decade of experience building software that people actually enjoy using. Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to work with both product and service-based teams, creating everything from large-scale consumer apps to enterprise solutions; many reaching millions of users.

I first started coding back in 2009, building apps for BlackBerry (yes, the trackball days!). Then came a brief stint experimenting with Nokia’s short-lived app platform using Qt, before finally finding my home in iOS development. Since then, I’ve worked extensively with Objective-C, Swift, SwiftUI, and more recently tinkered with Flutter, React and Next.js, and I enjoy learning new technologies and frameworks as they evolve.

Today, I provide custom software development services for businesses and individuals, specialising in mobile and web applications. Whether it's building new products, maintaining existing applications, implementing new features or solving technical challenges, I enjoy turning ideas into reliable, user-friendly software.

Beyond software development, I enjoy experimenting with Firebase, AI integrations and creating interactive animations. I'm a big fan of Batman and can usually be found rewatching Seinfeld or Supernatural when I'm not coding. If I'm not working on a project, I'm probably watching a good K-drama, catching a movie or spending time with my family.

I also enjoy documenting what I learn through technical articles, open-source projects and YouTube tutorials. This website is home to my blog, projects and experiments, with more of my work available on GitHub.

Want to connect? Feel free to reach out at [anupdsouza@gmail.com](mailto:anupdsouza@gmail.com), or check out what I'm currently building on GitHub.
